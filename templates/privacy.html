Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects personal financial information that you voluntarily enter into the application, including expense amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We also collect basic account information such as your email address and authentication credentials. All data is stored securely and used solely for the purpose of providing expense tracking functionality.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your expense data is used exclusively to provide and improve the Spendly service. We use your information to display expense summaries, generate category breakdowns, and enable filtering by time periods. We do not sell, share, or otherwise distribute your personal financial data to third parties for commercial purposes.

3. Data Storage

All expense data entered by users is stored in our secure database. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information, including encryption and access controls. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. We recommend maintaining your own backups of important financial data and using strong, unique passwords for your account.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, or other operational purposes. These third parties may have access to certain data as required to perform their functions, but are contractually obligated to maintain confidentiality and security. We do not share your personal financial data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, modify, or delete your personal data at any time. You can export your expense data through the application settings. If you choose to delete your account, all associated data will be permanently removed from our systems within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Continued use of the service after any such changes shall constitute your acceptance of the new policy. We encourage you to periodically review this page for the latest information on our privacy practices.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us through the application. We are committed to addressing any concerns you may have about your privacy.
